Getting started (three steps)

  1. Install the app from the App Market. Results come from Wix Site Search, a free Wix app; the dashboard's Setup card says Search index unavailable if it is missing.
  2. Add the search box. It is added automatically on install; to place it yourself, open the editor and choose Add Elements → App Widgets → Instant Site Search, then drop it into the header or any section.
  3. Publish the site. Products appear right away; new blog posts and pages usually show up within a minute of publishing.

Widget settings

Open the widget's settings panel in the editor; publish again after changing anything.

  • Placeholder text: the hint in the empty box. Default Search….
  • Accent color (hex): focus ring, highlights and links. Default #116dff.
  • Results per content type: 1 to 20, default 6; Show all opens the rest.
  • Search in: Products, Blog posts, Pages, Events, Services, Forum posts. At least one stays ticked.
  • Text language: Automatic (site language) or English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Português, 日本語, 한국어. Labels only; other languages fall back to English.
  • Show result images: thumbnails next to results. Pages that all share one image lose theirs.
  • Compact height: a 40 px box instead of 48 px.
  • Dark style (for dark sections): dark box and results panel.

How results work

The widget searches about a quarter of a second after typing stops, across every content type you ticked. Results are grouped by type (Products, Blog, Pages, Events, Services, Forum) with a match count; Show all N in Products → opens one type, 20 at a time, with Load more and ← All results.

Ranking starts from the index score and favours titles that equal, start with or contain the query; sold-out products show Sold out and rank lower. The index matches fuzzily, so small misspellings usually still work. A Korean query with no match is retried with the closest word from your content, shown as Showing results for: ….

On Pro the panel adds tabs (All plus one per matching type), Suggestions: chips while typing (featured terms, popular searches, then result titles; up to five), Try: chips in the empty box (up to eight) and a Popular right now list when nothing matched.

Dashboard

The app's page in your Wix dashboard shows Free plan or Pro plan in its header, next to Upgrade to Pro.

Analytics

Every plan sees the last 30 days; 90 days is Pro. Tiles show Searches, Zero-result rate, Avg. response and Unique queries, with tables of Top queries and Zero-result queries (with Map to a term shortcuts into synonyms). A weekly box reads This week: 42 searches (+7 vs. the week before) and names what found nothing. Export CSV (Pro) downloads top queries, zero-result queries and daily counts. Only the latest 5,000 searches count.

Synonyms (Pro)

Enter a Term and Synonyms (comma-separated), up to 20 per term, and click Add. Searching a term also searches its synonyms, and the other way round. Rows have an Enabled switch and Delete.

Featured suggestions (Pro)

Terms shown as visitors start typing and in the empty box; up to 20, 80 characters each. Click one to remove it.

Pinned results & hidden items (Pro)

Type a Search term, click Find items, then Pin for “term” to put an item first for that term or Hide to drop it from every result list. Up to 200 rules.

You can prepare synonyms, suggestions and rules on the Free plan; they start applying once the site is on Pro. Edits reach visitors within about a minute.

Free and Pro

Free: search across all six content types, grouping, show-all paging, every widget setting and 30 days of analytics.

Pro ($7.99/month, or $6.49/month billed yearly): synonyms, featured suggestions, autocomplete, the popular-searches fallback, result-type tabs, pinned results and hidden items, 90-day history and CSV export. Upgrade from the dashboard; the plan belongs to one site.

Privacy

Searches are logged to a Search Logs collection in your own CMS: query, result count, response time, content types and time. No visitor names, IP addresses or device identifiers are stored.

  • If the visitor declined analytics in your consent banner, the widget sends log=0: the search runs but is not recorded.
  • Queries over 60 characters or eight words, or containing links or markup characters, are never logged.
  • Logging stops at 60 searches per minute per site, so busy minutes are a lower bound.
  • A query becomes a public suggestion only after three or more searches on two different days.

Troubleshooting

  • Search is not set up for this site yet. — open the app dashboard once, then reload the page.
  • Too many searches. Please try again in a minute. — a rate limit; wait a minute.
  • This needs the CMS app on your site (free). — add CMS from the App Market and reload the dashboard.
