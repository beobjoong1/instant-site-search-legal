Instant Site Search for Shopify

Product search for your storefront. The price you see is the whole bill, and we never write to your theme.

What it does

  • Your catalog is indexed when you install, so search works as soon as setup is done.
  • Typo tolerant and accent aware — cafe finds café, shose finds shoes.
  • Set synonyms so the words shoppers actually type find the right products.
  • See which searches came up empty, so you know what your catalog is missing.

Four things we will not do

  • We never ask to edit your theme. The app does not request theme write access — you can check the permissions screen before you install. The search box is a theme app extension block that you place yourself.
  • No overage charges. No setup fees, no per-search fees, no "contact us" tier. Your price stays locked for as long as your subscription stays active.
  • Shoppers never see your plan. If you pass your plan's limit, analytics and new synonym rules pause — search keeps working exactly as before for shoppers.
  • If our servers go down, your search box does not. The widget falls back to Shopify's own search rather than showing an error.
